Our neighborhood story

About North Star Bakery

We began as a weekend market table shared by two neighborhood bakers. Today, our storefront is still guided by the same idea: careful baking can bring people together.

Ingredients with purpose

Thoughtful Sourcing

We choose regional flour when available, purchase seasonal fruit from nearby growers, and plan small daily batches to reduce waste. Our coffee and chocolate suppliers publish responsible sourcing standards.
